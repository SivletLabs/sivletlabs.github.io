Architecture & Theory

Why System-1 Policies Replace LLM Routing

Autoregressive models incur severe latency jitter (800ms+), GPU memory bandwidth bottlenecks, and token sampling errors when applied to discrete action spaces. SivletLabs formulates single-pass policy simplex networks trained with proper scoring rules.

01

Non-Autoregressive Policy Simplex

Discrete decisions do not require sequential token-by-token decoding. Sivlet-Decision-1 performs a single forward pass, evaluating all candidate actions simultaneously into a continuous probability simplex:

$$\pi_\theta(a \mid s) \in \Delta^{K-1}, \quad \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} \pi_\theta(c_k \mid s) = 1.0$$
Sub-15ms deterministic inference
02

Strictly Proper Scoring Rules

Standard RL scalar rewards lead to overconfidence. Sivlet models optimize under strictly proper scoring rules, mathematically incentivizing the policy to report its true posterior belief:

$$S_{\text{Brier}}(p, y^\star) = - \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} (p_k - \mathbb{I}[k = y^\star])^2$$
Calibrated probabilistic confidence
03

Gymnasium RL Harness (jev-eval)

Fully compliant with Farama Gymnasium APIs. Benchmarks agent decision policies across MDP simulation environments (Maze, Routing, Security, Snake) with Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE):

$$\hat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}(\gamma, \lambda)} = \sum_{l=0}^\infty (\gamma \lambda)^l \delta_{t+l}^V$$
